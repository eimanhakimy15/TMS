TMS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

TMS (Task Management System) is a personal task, project and notes dashboard created by Eiman Hakimy. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What we access

If you choose "Continue with Google", TMS asks for:

  • Your name and email address, only to show who is signed in.
  • Access to the hidden application data folder in your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). TMS can only see files that TMS itself created in that folder. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

How data is stored

Your tasks, projects, notes, vault entries and attachments are saved in your own Google Drive, in the app data folder. A temporary copy is kept in your browser while you are signed in, and it is removed when you sign out.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a server or database. Your data never passes through any server owned by us.
  • We do not sell, share or send your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data

You can remove TMS data at any time by revoking TMS access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the app's data from your Google Drive settings (Settings → Manage apps).
